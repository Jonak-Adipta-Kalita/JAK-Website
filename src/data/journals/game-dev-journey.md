- But the thing is, I just researched, didn't make much except a few from tutorials

### Godot (v3.5) from Heartbeast
- After a lot of thoughts of somehow starting game-dev from a proper game-engine, I made up my mind to start using Godot from a tutorial of [Heartbeast](https://youtube.com/@uheartbeast)
- I followed his RPG tutorial and made a pretty cool game. I didn't only do as much as he did in the tutorial, I added my own storyline and other elements to it... Really loved working on it
- Here it is btw (its too stupid ;-; idk why I released it): [https://jonak-adipta-kalita.itch.io/jak-ultimate-adventure-game](https://jonak-adipta-kalita.itch.io/jak-ultimate-adventure-game)

- Oh and, after I was done with 2D, I tried to create a low-poly 3D game... and it was soo shit... I learned a lot from it:
    - 2D is much more easier than 3D; coz, even though Pixel-Art or 2D Art in general is harder to make, 3D models, despite being Low-Poly are crazy hard
    - Lighting makes a huge difference
    - I am good in programming, soo bad in designing
- Anyways, if you wanna check it out for some reason.... then: [https://jonak-adipta-kalita.itch.io/jak-treasure-collectors-game](https://jonak-adipta-kalita.itch.io/jak-treasure-collectors-game)
