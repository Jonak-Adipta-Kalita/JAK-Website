## Backstory
- Shivangi Ma'am, our 9th grade Science teacher, asked us to make a project for Summer Vacation. And... I wanted to do something new and something that would challenge me.
- Over the 40 days, I brainstormed (well not much), and my tiny brain of 9th-grade thought of making an Electric Safe using Arduino..
- Yea I just wanted to make an IOT Project and this is what my brain could think of ;-;

## Building
- I collected all the electrical components with my uncle and the wood stuff with my dad at a carpenter's place.

- I remember that for atleast a week, all I would do is research in docs and write my code in vscode (yea, I had the Arduino IDE extension ehe)

- Github: [https://github.com/Jonak-Adipta-Kalita/JAK-Arduino-Electronic-Safe](https://github.com/Jonak-Adipta-Kalita/JAK-Arduino-Electronic-Safe)
- The Code: [https://github.com/Jonak-Adipta-Kalita/JAK-Arduino-Electronic-Safe/blob/master/main/main.ino](https://github.com/Jonak-Adipta-Kalita/JAK-Arduino-Electronic-Safe/blob/master/main/main.ino)

- Oh yea, the working? It opens with a Password and closes automatically after `1` minute, or when you press `C` on the Keypad.

### Components
- **Arduino Uno**: Microcontroller
- **Servo Motor**: Door Hinge
- **LCD Display (16x2)**: Display the Password typed in the Keypad
    - It wasn't working for a long time, turned out I had to adjust the visual contrast with the potentiometer on the backpack of the display 
- **Keypad (4x4)**: Press button to Enter the Password

- **Barrel Jack with 9V Battery Holder and Switch** (Custom made): Hold the 9V Battery and Power the Whole System
- **9V Battery**: Voltage Source

- **Plyboard**: To make the container
    - The carpenter was really helpful in it

- **Jumper Wires**: Connects the whole system together
    - I got a few wires soldered (well I didn't know how to back then so, I took help from an electrician) too tho

## Competition
- After the showcase at class, the project was kept in the science lab for like ages. When one day, Shivangi ma'am mentioned that she wants to submit my project for an exhibition
- It was called the *50th Jawaharlal Nehru Cluster Level Science, Mathematics and Environment Exhibition - 2022-23*, which was gonna happen at a different school.

- So, I fixed the project a bit (well some wires broke and the hinge needed re-tapping, and also heavy cleaning of dust and spider webs) and made it ready for the exhibition.
- I was in the ICT category. Got second position between 3 competitors.

- It was the experience of it that matters more than how the competition went ehe.
