## Backstory
- Mohit Sir was our Librarian in my Middle School and over the years, I grew very attached to him
- I was always sitting next to him in the library, and literally worked as his "assistant"
- Like, I can't put more emphasis on how much I was attached to him; To this day, he is the best teacher I have ever met
- Well, like any other faculty in a government school, he had to take a transfer to a different place.
- This is how I said goodbye to him

- Actually I wanna say a bit more thing, I remember vividly the day he told me that he was getting the transfer.
- It was a Saturday, I locked the library after working on the website and went to the Office Room to give the keys to him.
- He was printing some paper; which he showed to me when I gave him the key. It was a transfer certificate of him to a much bigger institution. Yes, I was the first to know at school. Yup.
- I wanted to cry so badly but I didn't let any emotions out coz he told me to not tell anyone.

- It took some time for me to process what happened and boi I was crying like a baby on the Sunday that fall between the Monday and the Saturday
- I remember I cried like 8 times with my face burried in my pillow at the thought of never being able to see him for a long time.

- I cried more on the day it was announced on stage that he was getting transfered. I was so teary that I couldn't see anything while playing keyboard for national anthem.
- I remember crying while hugging him.

- I made the website in the 2 days I got after I learned about the news.
- Man I will never forget the smile and him getting emotional when I opened the site in our library chromebook and showed it to him.

## Development of the Website
- Used React with Vite and TailwindCSS
- I had like a few hours to work on this coz I was tryna write the letter too. The letter took the most amount of time
- I also asked sir for some of his pics so that I could put it on there

- I was very bad at quick UI designs I suppose coz the color scheme I picked is so bad. But, its the emotions that I made the thing in 9th grade that matters
- I really don't remember why I picked the orange background and darkblue foreground... but its a vibe

- Yea, I had to put an emphasis on the fact that I was his "Assistant" \<3
