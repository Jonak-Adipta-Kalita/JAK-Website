## Early Introduction
- I don't remember the exact time when it happened, but I think this is my first exposure to when I first got my interest in electronics
- I was spending my holidays at my Mom's Brother place in like \<5th grade. My cousin in one of those days, came to me with a motor and an 9v battery (that he got his dad to buy) and said, "lets make a fan"
- Over the next 2 days, we used papers and cardboards to build the entire thing, and after a lot of trial and errors, got a simple DC motor hooked to a power source :D

- I repeated this Fan-Project repeatedly in the next few years (a lot in lockdown)... probably like 5 times, sourcing materials from old electronics like my RC Car
- I must say, the last Fan-Project I did, was so good in its external strucuture. I also got other components like Switches and LEDs connected (although the LEDs died out everytime coz I didn't put any resistances)

- Then in 6th grade, we made (tried) a Smart-Dustbin with Arduino as a class project as I have mentioned in -> [[programming-journey|My Programming Journey]]
- (that was a disaster... but fun ehe)

- In 9th Grade, I made the [[jak-arduino-electronic-safe|Electronic Safe Project]] with an Arduino :D

- I did have more IOT ideas later in the highschool years but, I couldn't get myself to actually work on it ;-;
- Oh year, I did get the Sprig and Blot from [Hackclub](https://hackclub.com/) in 10th Grade :D | And also a raspberry pi pico from them.
